Who runs Salas 64?

My name is Steven Salas. I’m a 16-year-old from Pasadena, California, and I update the Salas 64 rankings every day during the college basketball season.

What is Salas 64?

Salas 64 is my own top-64 ranking of college basketball teams. It’s built to track the teams I believe matter most for March – not just by record, but by how good they really are and who they’ve beaten.

Why I made these rankings

The main goal is to help people build better March Madness brackets. By following a daily updated top-64, you can:

  • See which teams are truly trending up or down before the bracket is picked.
  • Spot underrated teams that might be better than their seed line.
  • Get a clearer picture of which wins and losses actually matter.

How to use Salas 64

  • Check the daily rankings to see where your team stands and who’s moving.
  • Use the list as a guide when you’re filling out your March Madness bracket.
  • Follow the game listings and predictions to keep track of key matchups for ranked teams.

Update schedule

I aim to update the rankings every day, especially after busy slates and big results that affect the top of the board or bubble-type teams.
