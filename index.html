How the challenge works

Top 25 matchups take priority, followed by games with the best combined Salas ranks. The slate is fixed when published. Salas picks use the simulator's adjusted efficiency formula plus a 3-point home-court adjustment, and are frozen before tipoff. Percentages are model estimates, not proven accuracy. Only final games you picked count in the comparison. Missed, canceled, and postponed games do not count. Results refresh periodically, so grading may lag the final whistle. The challenge day follows Pacific time; tipoff times appear in your local time.
